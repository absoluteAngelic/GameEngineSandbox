# GameEngineSandbox

Created a super short and basic platformer to demonstrate unity understanding and speed of development. There's a player that can jump and move in 4 directions, by touching the final cube you win. There's a win condition, lose condition, and player controller. It's in 3D with a over-the-shoulder camera. 

Checklist:
-Player that can be controlled
-Platforms
-Jumping and movement with rigidbody physics
-Lose when falling off the platforms
-Win when touching the last platform
